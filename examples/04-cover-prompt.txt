Soft daylight in an old tailor's workshop with tall windows, honey-oak cutting tables and bolts of wool on shelves. A middle-aged woman tailor with freckles, fine wrinkles and loose grey hair stands next to a glossy white plastic mannequin, both wearing identical tweed jackets.
